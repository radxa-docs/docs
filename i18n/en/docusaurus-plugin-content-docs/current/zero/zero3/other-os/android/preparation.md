## Development preparation

### Power

The Radxa ZERO 3W / 3E is powered via the **USB-C 1** port (USB 2.0 OTG) and only supports 5V input. A 5V/2A or higher power adapter is recommended.

:::tip
Radxa recommends [Radxa Power PD30W](../../accessories/pd-30w).
:::
